CRISPR HTS Validator — analyse pooled and arrayed CRISPR screens, with a quality check at every step
by Paul H. Kim · https://paul-h-kim.com/software/crispr-hts-validator

1. Drag "CRISPR HTS Validator.app" into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).

2. Double-click it. Because the app is free and not registered with Apple, macOS
   may say it "cannot be opened" or "cannot verify the developer" the first time:
     - Click Done (or OK).
     - Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway" next to CRISPR HTS Validator. Confirm with your password.
     - On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click (Control-click) the app,
       choose Open, then click Open.
   You only need to do this once.

3. The first start sets up Python's scientific libraries (numpy, pandas, scipy,
   statsmodels, matplotlib and others) in
   ~/Library/Application Support/CRISPR HTS Validator. This needs internet once
   and takes a minute or two. The app then opens in your web browser.

Needs: a Mac with macOS 12 or newer and Python 3.9 or newer. Most Macs already
have it; if not, macOS offers to install the "command line developer tools"
(click Install), or get Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/ .

Everything runs on your Mac: your screen data is never uploaded. Closing the
browser tab quits the app. Example screens (Hart lab HAP1 TKOv3, MAGeCK demos,
cellHTS2 KcViab) are included, each under its own licence (in the app's
test-data folder).
